## Professional Statement


I am an aspiring cybersecurity professional with a background in web development and UI/UX design and a strong passion for learning and developing my skills in cybersecurity. I am particularly interested in protecting people, organizations, and sensitive information from cyber threats and unauthorized access.

My strengths include problem-solving, attention to detail, web development, UI/UX design, and a willingness to continuously learn new technologies and security practices. As I transition into the cybersecurity field, I am developing my knowledge of security principles, risk management, threat detection, and best practices for protecting digital assets.

I value integrity, following laws and regulations, and ensuring that technology is used responsibly and equitably. I believe that cybersecurity professionals have a responsibility to protect information while maintaining trust and respecting users' rights.

Through the Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate program and continued hands-on learning, I am building the technical knowledge and practical skills needed to begin a career in cybersecurity. My goal is to contribute to a security team by helping identify vulnerabilities, reduce security risks, protect sensitive information, and support an organization's overall security objectives.
